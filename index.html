My first steps in web development

September 27, 2026 by Javonte Anthony

This week I built my first blog page. Starting with a few HTML elements helped me see how a document becomes a website. Each heading, paragraph and image has a place in the story I want to share. Building a solid semantic structure ensures that content is accessible and well-organized for all users.

CSS made the biggest visual difference. I experimented with spacing, colours and typography, then used Grid to arrange the main content and sidebar. Small changes made the page much easier to read. Working with layouts gives you direct feedback on how design principles shape modern web interfaces.

Finding inspiration outside

September 29, 2026 by Javonte Anthony

A walk outside is a good way to reset after working at a screen. This weekend I noticed how the landscape combines simple shapes and colours. It gave me a few ideas for my next design. Taking breaks away from code allows creative problems to solve themselves in the background.

Back at my desk, I tried to bring that sense of calm into this page. A little more space between sections and a simpler sidebar helped. I am learning that good design gives each element room to breathe. Balancing visual minimalism with rich content is one of the most rewarding aspects of frontend engineering.
